## About

Continuum Bio Analysis is a web-based structural perturbation analysis tool for comparing the modeled effects of medical conditions and compounds using the SEAM v3.12 locked runtime.

- **Frontend**: Single-page HTML/CSS/JavaScript application (public, in this repository)
- **Backend**: Proprietary SEAM engine (in private repository: [seam-private-engines](https://github.com/jlanjou-SEAM/seam-private-engines/tree/main/bio-analysis))
- **Data**: Compound and condition databases (public, in this repository)
- **Purpose**: Educational and research use only; results are not medical advice

## Using the Published Site

The live page at the link above is a **frontend only**. To use it, you need either:

1. **Access to a deployed backend** (contact maintainers for URL)
2. **Run the backend locally** — clone [seam-private-engines](https://github.com/jlanjou-SEAM/seam-private-engines) and follow instructions in `bio-analysis/README.md`
