# Claim Boundary

This archive documents a **structural perturbation cause-and-effect assessment**.

Supported by the recorded calculation:

- famotidine alone perturbs the represented language/speech structural family;
- NH3 alone can perturb the same represented family;
- the joint famotidine + NH3 state produces a larger displacement of that family than famotidine alone at the tested retained-burden coordinates;
- the displacement increases across the tested NH3 series;
- the result arises without inserting manufacturer adverse-event labels into the engine.

Not established by this calculation alone:

- that NH3 is the only upstream contributor to clinical CNS events;
- that a particular patient's liver or kidney state corresponds to one of the test burden coordinates;
- that the declared NH3 burden values are clinical blood concentrations or treatment doses;
- that this calculation replaces clinical pharmacology, toxicology, or patient-specific evaluation.

Manufacturer warnings and conventional adverse-event observations can be compared with these structural results downstream, but they are not part of the engine calculation preserved here.
